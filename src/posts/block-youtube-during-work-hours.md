# How to schedule YouTube blocking for work or study

Set a schedule for the hours when you want YouTube out of the way. Intentional YT lets you choose the days and decide whether to lock the site or leave videos available under stricter protections.

I'm Manas, Intentional YT's developer. This guide covers the YouTube website in desktop Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.

## Choose what happens during scheduled hours

**Block YouTube entirely** shows a locked commitment screen during the hours you selected. Choose it when you do not want to watch YouTube at all in that window.

**Force all protections on** turns on every block control during the schedule, including settings you normally leave off. Playback remains available, although YouTube may look more stripped down than usual. When the schedule ends, your regular settings return.

## Create a YouTube work-hours schedule

1. [Install Intentional YT](/) in the browser where you use YouTube. Open the extension and select **Focus**.
2. Open **Scheduled Focus Sessions** and switch on **Scheduled Blocking**.
3. Select **+ Add Schedule**. Give the rule a label, such as **Work hours** or **Study time**.
4. Choose the active days and set a start and end time. The form starts with Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 selected. Times follow your computer's local clock.
5. Under **Blocking Mode**, choose **Block YouTube entirely** or **Force all protections on**, then select **Save Schedule**.
6. Make sure the schedule's own switch and the main **Scheduled Blocking** switch are both on.

![Intentional YT Focus tab showing Scheduled Blocking enabled with a Monday to Friday study schedule from 09:00 to 12:00](/screenshots/product-schedule.webp "A weekday study schedule in the Focus tab")

You can create another rule for a different block, such as an afternoon study session. Each rule has its own days, hours, and mode.

To cover a session that ends the next morning, set an end time earlier than the start time. The selected day is the day the schedule begins.

## What happens outside the schedule?

When the scheduled hours end, your usual block settings resume. A daily playback limit runs independently, so it can still pause a video later in the day.

If a schedule does not start, check that the extension is enabled, YouTube protections are not snoozed, and both schedule switches are on. Reload YouTube if the tab was open when you changed the settings.

## Schedule or daily time limit?

Use a schedule to protect particular hours, such as weekday mornings. A daily limit sets how long you can watch in total. You can use both: the schedule covers work or study hours, while the daily limit caps playback for the rest of the day. See the [daily YouTube time-limit guide](/blog/set-youtube-daily-time-limit) to set a budget.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I schedule YouTube blocking for weekdays only?

Yes. The form starts with weekdays selected. Use **Active Days** to choose Saturday, Sunday, or another combination.

### Can I still watch a video during a scheduled block?

Yes, with **Force all protections on**. It hides distracting parts of YouTube while leaving playback available. **Block YouTube entirely** shows a locked commitment screen during the scheduled hours.

### Does the schedule block YouTube on my phone?

No. A schedule applies to the desktop browser profile where you installed Intentional YT. It does not affect the YouTube phone app or a different browser profile.

### How do I stop the schedule?

Use the switch on a schedule card to pause that rule. Use the main **Scheduled Blocking** switch to pause all rules. Your saved schedules stay in place.
